\appendix{Stakeholder Questionnaire Instruments}
\label{app:questionnaires}

Between 2 and 7 December 2025, three separate questionnaires were distributed to the three stakeholder groups using Google Forms. Each instrument was tailored to the specific role and workflows of its target group while covering shared themes: current service delivery pain points, connectivity conditions, technology readiness, and digital platform expectations. Across all three surveys, a total of 49 responses were collected (see Table~\ref{tab:survey-respondents} in Chapter~\ref{ch:methodology}).

\appendix{System Usability Scale (SUS) Instrument and Score Calculation}
\label{app:sus}

The System Usability Scale (SUS) \citep{Brooke1996} consists of ten items, each rated on a five-point scale (\textbf{1} = Strongly Disagree, \textbf{5} = Strongly Agree), with positive and negative items alternating to reduce response bias. The instrument was administered to all nine participants (Table~\ref{tab:sus-participants}) on 10 February 2026.

\subsection*{Scoring Formula}

Let $r_i$ be a participant's response to item $i$. The per-item contribution $x_i$ is:
\[
x_i =
\begin{cases}
  r_i - 1 & \text{odd items } (i = 1,3,5,7,9) \\
  5 - r_i & \text{even items } (i = 2,4,6,8,10)
\end{cases}
\]
The individual SUS score is $S = 2.5 \times \displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{10} x_i\,$, giving a value in $[0,\,100]$.

\subsection*{Group Mean Calculation}

Applying the formula to each participant (P1--P9) yields the individual scores shown in Table~\ref{tab:sus-participants}. The group mean is:
\[
\bar{S}
  = \frac{\displaystyle\sum_{p=1}^{9} S_p}{9}
  = \frac{77.5 + 72.5 + 75.0 + 70.0 + 75.0 + 77.5 + 72.5 + 72.5 + 77.5}{9}
  = \frac{670.5}{9}
  \approx \mathbf{74.5}
\]
Following \citet{Bangor2009}, a score of 74.5 falls in the \textbf{``Good''} usability band and exceeds the widely cited industry average of 68.
